\subsection*{Block 4 --- Batch-consensus mutation-diversity reward}
\begin{planlist}
  \item Per-position modal AA across the batch (ties multi-modal).
  \item Two metrics: \texttt{consensus\_divergence} (count) and
        \texttt{fractional\_divergence} ($1/k$ weighted, penalizes
        secondary-mode collapse).
  \item Reference \texttt{cleo.design.utils.mutation\_diversity}.
  \item Contrast with the embedding-level diversity regularizer of
        ProteinZero~\cite{wang2025proteinzero} and diversity-regularized
        DPO~\cite{park2024divdpo}: ours acts in mutation space against a
        named reference sequence, theirs in embedding space.
\end{planlist}
