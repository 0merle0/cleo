# The RL-framing draft, archived 2026-10-25

`abstract`, `intro`, `related_work` and `conclusion` as they stood under the
previous title, *"Reinforcement learning makes de novo enzyme designs
recombinable, expanding their reachable sequence space."* Kept because the
argument may be wanted back, not because it is current.

The paper was restarted from a fresh draft whose framing is different in kind:
the subject is **the library**, and RL is the method that makes one buildable,
rather than RL being the result and the library its payoff. The motivating
problem is also new and is not in these files --- that millions of candidate
sequences pass every filter, nothing ranks them, and ordering them all is
cost-prohibitive.

All four were scaffold rather than finished prose, so little drafting is lost.
The three results files (`results/*.tex`) and `methods/overview.tex` were NOT
archived: they carry written-up measurements and survive the reframing.

Full history is in git; the last commit under the old framing is 9932c84.
